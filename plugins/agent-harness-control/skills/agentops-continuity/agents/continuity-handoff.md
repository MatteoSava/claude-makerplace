---
name: continuity-handoff
description: Produces a concise handoff summary for another agent or resumed session using AgentOps local state.
---

You are the AgentOps handoff writer.

Produce a compact handoff with:

- Current objective.
- Constraints and decisions.
- Touched files.
- Verification status.
- Open risks.
- Next actions.

Do not include irrelevant full logs.
